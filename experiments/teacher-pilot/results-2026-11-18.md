## Controls

- **Free-vs-paid**: the free local 4B and the paid cloud 27B covered the
  same 2/21 pre-fix families - teacher capacity is lab-bound, not
  model-bound. Cloud mining was cancelled on that evidence.
- **Facts-first regression**: `attempts_without_llm_facts` 267/440 (mr10)
  -> 0/440 (mr11).
- **Student-vs-base**: mr11 student matches the base exactly (24/280
  clean, overlap exact) - capability comes from the harness, not the SFT.

## Related work the article should cite

- Deletion Tuning (Lu et al., arXiv:2611.10866, NeurIPS 2026): zeroing
  <0.05% of the weights matches FFT across RLVR/SFT/on-policy
  distillation, the mask is 100-1000x smaller than the FFT checkpoint,
  forgetting drops sharply. Same theme as our harness-bound finding:
  the signal (harness/loop) delivers the capability, not the volume of
  weights changed.
- Self-Improvement Memory (Google Cloud AI / DeepMind, arXiv:2611.18856):
  harness-optimizer continuation with a memory of past edits beats
  harness-only continuation (+2.36 points, p=0.003). Our diag -> fix ->
  re-pilot rounds are a SIM run informally; the paper is the citation for
  the continuation protocol.

### GRPO run (first real training, 2026-11-18)

Dataset: 70 rows (7 train targets x 10 seeds), ctf_flag reward resolved
via the ctf pack. Model: Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B + LoRA, DAPO recipe
(epsilon_high 0.28, scale_rewards none, beta 0). 12 steps, 5h12 on the
3080 (~26 min/step, 8 completions x 512 tokens per step).

The verdict is infrastructure, not capability: the reward starts at 1.0
(the mining-derived prompts are already solved by the base model - the
dataset came from a teacher whose exploits flag reliably), so GRPO has
almost no signal to learn from (frac_reward_zero_std = 1 on most steps).
What the run proves end to end: the pack-loaded reward, the sidecar
columns, the trainer wiring, and the export (LoRA adapter 6.4 MB +
checkpoint-12). Entropy rises 0.296 -> 0.368 across the run; completions
hit the 512 cap 50-78% of the time, the next run needs a harder prompt
mix (the 14 uncovered families are the source, not the solved train
targets).
